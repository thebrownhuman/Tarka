🎯 Standardized Coding-Decoding Logic Taxonomy

Coding-decoding items evaluate candidate competency across six core mathematical and logical transformation classes. Mastery of these operational templates drastically reduces item resolution times on standardized entrance exams.

1. Constant & Progressive Alphabetical Shifts

Items 1-4, 31-32

Letters are shifted forward (+k) or backward (-k) along the 26-letter English alphabet. Shifts can be uniform across all positions or follow an index-dependent linear sequence ($+1, +2, +3 \dots$).

Mechanism: Pos(i) → (Pos(i) ± k) mod 26
Common Distractor: Boundary wrap-around mistakes (Z → A) and off-by-one arithmetic slips.

2. Reversals & Spatial Permutations

Items 5-7, 24, 40, 66

Letter order is manipulated without altering underlying character identities. Includes exact sequence reversal, adjacent pairwise swapping, split-half reversal, block rearrangement, and index permutations.

Mechanism: Index(i) → Index(f(i))
Common Distractor: Inverting encoding direction vs decoding direction.

3. Positional Arithmetic & Scalar Values

Items 8-12, 27-28, 45-47

Alphabetical positions (1-indexed forward: A=1, Z=26; reverse: A=26, Z=1) are converted into scalar numerical codes via summation, vowel/consonant count multipliers, or boundary products.

Mechanism: Sum(Forward Pos) × Multiplier or Reverse Pos (27 - Pos)
Common Distractor: Miscalculating reverse positions or failing to account for double-counted vowels.

4. Alphabet Mirroring & Direct Substitution

Items 10, 13, 33-34, 36

Characters map directly to their alphabet mirror pair where forward and backward positions sum to 27 ($A \leftrightarrow Z, B \leftrightarrow Y, C \leftrightarrow X$), or follow direct symbol key tables.

Mechanism: Mirror Letter Pos = 27 - Current Pos
Common Distractor: Mirroring consonants while forgetting vowel-fixation exceptions.

5. Non-Linear Series & Mathematical Offsets

Items 41-44, 51, 80, 90

Shift offsets increase non-linearly across letter indices according to well-known mathematical sequences: Fibonacci numbers (+1, +1, +2, +3, +5), Prime numbers (+2, +3, +5, +7, +11), Perfect Squares (+1, +4, +9, +16), or Triangular numbers (+1, +3, +6, +10).

Mechanism: Pos(i) → Pos(i) + Sequence(i)
Common Distractor: Misidentifying 1 as a prime number or applying incorrect Fibonacci seeds.

6. Multi-Stage Conditional & Algebraic Ciphers

Items 81-100

Advanced multi-stage transformations where logical branching occurs based on vowel-consonant counts, index parity, polyalphabetic keywords ("KEY"), modular algebraic equations ($(Pos \times i) \bmod 26$), or base conversions (Base-8 Octal).

Mechanism: IF (Consonants > Vowels) → Shift A ELSE Shift B
Common Distractor: Failing to apply conditional forks or missing sub-stage reversals.
